YOUR SYSTEM FOR TODAY

Do these 5 things in this order.

You do not need to transform your life today. Follow the sequence, collect evidence, and stop when the day is done.

5 MIN · START
1. Choose your focus

Read today’s mental model and write its meaning in your own words.

Example: If today is First Principles, separate facts from assumptions about one challenge at work.
45–60 MIN · DEEP WORK
2. Practise on one real problem

Put your phone away. Work on one meaningful task without switching tabs or chasing a perfect plan.

Example: Sketch a solution, list constraints, implement one part, or solve one interview problem.
5 MIN · MIDDAY
3. Pause and apply the model

Notice one decision, frustration or assumption. Use the model's question before reacting.

Example: Write what you know, what you are assuming, and what evidence is missing.
10 MIN · ACTION
4. Do one thing you’ve been avoiding

Choose a small action that moves your real life forward. Make it concrete and finishable.

Example: Ask for feedback, clarify a requirement, or explain your design to a teammate.
10 MIN · EVENING
5. Review without self-judgment

Write one thing you did, one thing you learned, and one adjustment for tomorrow.

Example: “I focused for 40 minutes. I got stuck on X. Tomorrow I’ll start by testing Y.”
Rule for today: one real action beats ten hours of thinking about becoming a better person.
